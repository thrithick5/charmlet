Questions

An honest FAQ

Will I be luckier?
No guaranteed luck or productivity benefit. It is an optional desktop decoration with personal meaning and playful interactions — a little luck, kept close, measured entirely by how it makes you feel.
Will it get in the way of my clicks?
No. The window is click-through everywhere except the charm itself. It never steals focus. Clicks on browser tabs, your IDE, everything underneath, pass straight through.
Can I use this on Windows?
Yes! You can download the 1-click Windows installer (Charmlet-Setup.exe) directly from the Install section. It installs in seconds without requiring administrator privileges, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts with a dedicated charm icon, and runs cleanly in a dedicated app window.
Why is it free?
Because this is a personal build for one laptop, not a store product. There is nothing to pay for and nothing to upsell — no licence server, no account, no checkout. The charm is simply yours.
Does it need the internet?
No. The page you are reading works offline, the app works offline, and your charm choice lives in preferences on this machine. Nothing is sent anywhere, ever.
Does it slow my computer down?
The app uses resources to draw and animate the charm. Usage depends on your computer and activity; the Verlet solver is deliberately lightweight, but we do not promise zero CPU use or a particular battery impact.
Where is my data?
In UserDefaults on this Mac: which charm is hanging, whether it is visible, and where on the top edge it hangs. Delete the app's preferences and it forgets everything. Nothing is sent to any server.
Is the rope real physics?
Yes — a position-based Verlet solver. Each node stores only its current and previous position, distance constraints are relaxed every frame, and the cord never stretches no matter how hard you fling it. The same engine runs here and inside the app, which is why the physics on this page is not a video.
