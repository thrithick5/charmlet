Questions

The honest FAQ

Is this Spider-Man?
No, and it deliberately is not. Spider-Man is Marvel and Disney intellectual property, so a product shipping his name and suit would be infringement and would be taken down on day one. The hero here is The Slinger — an original masked web-slinger drawn from scratch as SVG for this project, with his own proportions, his own lenses and his own mark. He hangs upside down because that is the whole joke, not because he is anyone else's character. If you own licensed artwork, the charm is a single file and you can drop your own in without touching the engine.
Does it block my clicks?
No. The overlay ignores mouse events entirely until your pointer is genuinely inside the charm's own hit area — then it lets go again the moment you leave. Clicks on browser tabs, IDE menus and game crosshairs all pass straight through. That is implemented with a global mouse-moved monitor flipping the window's ignoresMouseEvents flag, because a full-screen overlay that swallowed every click would be unusable.
Why does it not stretch when I drag it hard?
The cord is a position-based Verlet solver rather than a spring simulation. Each node stores its current and previous position, and distance constraints are relaxed repeatedly each frame, so the cord stays rigid no matter how violently you fling it. The same solver runs on this page and inside the app, which is why the physics here is not a video or a mock-up — it is the actual engine.
Can I add my own charm?
Yes. A charm is one object in web/js/charms.js: an id, a name, a plaque, an inline SVG with data-part tags on the pieces you want to animate, and a ritual() function. Add it to the CHARMS array and it appears in the gallery, on the site and in the app's menu, because both hosts share the same engine.
Why is the sound off when I arrive?
Because a website that makes noise before you ask is a bad website. Every sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API — thwips, keycap clacks, temple bells, slurps, squishes — so there are no audio files to download and nothing to license. The speaker button in the header turns it on.
What does the aura meter actually measure?
Your own flicks, taps and rituals in this browser session, stored in localStorage. It is not a global counter, it is not sent anywhere, and no other visitor is included in it. It is a joke about aura farming that happens to be completely truthful.
Refunds?
If it does not bring you luck, or you simply do not like it, ask within 30 days and you get your money back. The luck clause is not enforceable, so the refund clause covers it instead.
Is the checkout live?
Not yet. The pricing buttons are wired to a stub that tells you so rather than pretending to take a card. Connect a Stripe payment link and they work as-is.
